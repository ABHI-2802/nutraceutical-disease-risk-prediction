⚠️ Critical Caveats

Model Limitations & Clinical Scope

  1. 1
    Measurement variability: Stress, caffeine, exercise and measurement technique can alter readings and affect model scores.
  2. 2
    Input uncertainty: Inaccurate dietary estimates, incorrect units or confusion with blood-test values can affect predictions.
  3. 3
    Incomplete medical context: The model does not fully account for symptoms, medications, family history and clinical examination findings.
  4. 4
    Limited validation: Trained on U.S. adult data, the model remains unvalidated for Indian populations and provides research scores rather than diagnoses.
